About

A visual director with a cinema eye and a designer's rigor.

Over fifteen years, I've shaped the visual language of luxury fashion houses, independent film productions, and global technology brands — distilling complex stories into images that are immediately felt before they are understood.

Based between Tokyo and Amsterdam, I bring an obsessive attention to composition, colour, and silence. My work lives at the intersection of editorial precision and cinematic atmosphere.

How I work

Four stages.
One clear outcome.

01

Immersion

Two weeks of deep research — studying your history, your audience, the culture you're speaking into, and the competitors you're trying to leave behind.

02

Direction

A single clear creative brief. One direction — not three options. The brief becomes the north star for every subsequent decision.

03

Production

Meticulous craft. Whether it's a shoot, a film, or a book — every element is made with the same attention. Nothing placeholder, nothing temporary.

04

Delivery

Comprehensive handover — master files, usage guidelines, and a conversation about what comes next. The relationship doesn't end at invoice.
